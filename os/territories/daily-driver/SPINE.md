# SPINE — the daily driver the user calls MINE

The goal is not "big." The goal is not "deep." The goal is the sentence people
actually say, in exactly these five words:

    "that is my computer."

That is the entire product. KorrinOS wins when a person uses it every day,
trusts it, and calls it theirs — with the same weight they say it about the
thing on their desk at work. Every slice, every world, every byte exists to
make that sentence true. Nothing else matters.

## what "mine" means (the honesty bar)
- "mine" is earned daily, not claimed. You do not call a thing yours the day
  you install it. You call it yours the 60th day, when it just worked again
  and you reached for it without thinking.
- KorrinOS therefore tracks DAILY USE as the only metric that cannot be padded.
  No benchmark, no ISO size, no feature count. Only: did it get used today.
- The journal below is the honest log of that. Every entry is a real session,
  real bytes, real work — the actual thing we reached for today. padding is
  forbidden by AGENTS.md; so is a journal that pretends to be a day it wasn't.
